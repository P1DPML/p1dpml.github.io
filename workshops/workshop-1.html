Workshop 1

DP and Unlearning in ML

June 11-12, 2026 · Day 1: 10:30-16:45 · Day 2: 09:00-15:45

Overview

Workshop 1 is part of the Data Privacy in Machine Learning P1 program. The workshop will bring together tutorials, invited talks, and contributed talks on differential privacy, machine unlearning, and privacy-preserving machine learning.

The program will include a differential privacy tutorial on the first day and a machine unlearning tutorial on the second day. Confirmed invited speakers include Antti Honkela and Graham Cormode. The call for contributed talks will be announced as confirmations are finalized.
